På dansk ↓

  • SeasonsEvery season’s calendar, standings and a chart of the title fight.
  • RacesResults, sprint, qualifying, grid and pit stops, with the podium and track layout.
  • DriversCareer totals, wins and podiums per season, retirements and penalties.
  • ConstructorsEvery team’s history, including the names it has raced under.
  • CircuitsAll circuits with every historic layout, maps and most successful drivers.
  • RecordsAll-time lists: most wins, youngest winners, most DNFs and much more.

The statistics live on your device and work offline, and new results are downloaded automatically after each race weekend. No account, no ads, no tracking. Available in English and Danish, on iPhone and iPad.
